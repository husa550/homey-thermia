Your heat pump is one of the hardest-working parts of your home, yet it usually lives in its own little world. This app brings it into Homey so it can finally play along with everything else. Keep an eye on how the whole system is doing, from outdoor, brine and hot water temperatures to the current operating status, all in one place.

From there it is up to your imagination: nudge the room temperature or give the hot water a boost from your phone, ease off the heating when electricity is expensive, get a reminder when the compressor is due for service, or let any change in the pump set a Flow in motion.

Developed and verified with a Thermia Atlas. Other heat pumps connected to Thermia Online should work as well, although the available readings and controls can differ from model to model.
